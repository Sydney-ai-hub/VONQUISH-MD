
# 📱 VONQUISH MD – Pairing Guide

Welcome to VONQUISH MD!

Follow these steps to connect your WhatsApp account.

## 🔐 How to Get a Pairing Code

1. Open the VONQUISH MD project.
2. Install Node.js on your computer.
3. Download or clone this repository.
4. Open the project folder in your terminal.
5. Install dependencies using:

   `npm install`

6. Start the bot using:

   `node index.js`

7. Enter your own WhatsApp number when prompted.
8. Follow the terminal instructions to request a pairing code.
9. Open WhatsApp on your phone.
10. Go to **Linked Devices**.
11. Select **Link a Device**.
12. Choose **Link with phone number instead**.
13. Enter the pairing code shown in your terminal.

## ⚠️ Important

- Only connect a WhatsApp account you own or have permission to use.
- Never share your pairing code with anyone.
- Never share your WhatsApp session files.
- Pairing is experimental and may not work reliably yet.
